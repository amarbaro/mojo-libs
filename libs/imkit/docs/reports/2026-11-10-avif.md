## Stage 3 notes

- CDF storage is libaom's inverse form: entry i holds `32768 - cdf[i]` (the spec's `f`), entry N - 1 is 0, entry N is the
  adaptation counter. The default-table converter (a later stage) must write this form.
- The gate encodes random streams (1 to 4 CDFs of N = 2..16 with random start counters, booleans, literals of 1..16 bits;
  every fifth stream with CDF update disabled) with `tests/av1sym_enc.mojo`, a transcription of libaom's `od_ec_enc`, and
  decodes them with the spec-8.2 transcription under test. After every symbol the decoder's CDF must equal the spec-convention
  CDF adapted by the encoder's own `update`, and `exit_process()` must accept the trailing bits.
- Encoder and decoder come from two different sources (libaom code, AV1 spec text), so agreement checks both. Real tile
  data is checked by the stage 4 gate.
- The DeepSeek draft passed unchanged; no fixes were needed.
