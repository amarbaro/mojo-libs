## What it is

Baseline, extended-sequential and progressive Huffman (all four progressive scan kinds, including the AC refinement), 8-bit, 1 or 3 components, any 1 to 4 sampling factors, restart markers, APPn and COM skipped, Adobe transform flag and JFIF read for the RGB-or-YCbCr call. The IDCT is libjpeg's jidctint (one 1-D routine for both passes), upsampling is libjpeg's "fancy" triangle filter for h2v1 / h2v2 / h1v2 and replication for the rest, YCbCr uses libjpeg's 16-bit fixed-point constants. Baseline blocks go through the IDCT as they are decoded (no coefficient buffer); progressive files keep Int16 coefficients until the last scan.
Bad input: every segment length is checked, the 64 M pixel guard (`MAXPX`, same value as `assets.MAXPX`, asserted by the test) runs before any allocation, a Huffman table that is over-subscribed raises, and a truncated entropy stream raises because the bit reader feeds virtual zero bits after a marker or the end and raises when one is consumed.

## Not covered

- No fixture with 4:1:1 or 4:1:0 or with odd factors (3x1): the replicate path is exercised only by the sampling-factor fuzz (decodes or raises, output not compared).
- No CMYK, arithmetic, 12-bit, lossless, DNL, multiple-frame files: they raise, and the raise is tested for 4 components, 12-bit and SOF9 only.
- No Adobe-RGB (transform 0) fixture: ImageMagick would not write one; that branch is read by eye only.
- The decoder is not tuned; 19.6 ms is the first correct version.
