# oracle: numpy float64 brute force with the same tie rule (cosine rounded to 1e-9, then lower row), tools/oracle_cv2.py "embedding index".
# Fixtures are 768-wide vectors from the oracle's own seed 211, not pictures: emb_tw_q holds 40 queries that are an item plus noise of
# sigma 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5 (cosine .995 to .89, standing for crop, rescale, JPEG, colour) among 20 wrong items at cosine .9 with a
# true item; emb_dd holds 40 same-item rows (cosine .9988) and 20 look-alike rows (cosine .96). Not built: twins from real pictures
# (needs SigLIP runs), false pairs on the 46 accepted stock items (labels not on disk), the timed 4,202 x 768 query (boss timing run).
