# Provenance: 2D vision follows OpenCV 5.0 semantics, so cv2 is the oracle and the bar

Everything this lane builds has a cv2 5.0 function with the same meaning: match its output within the case
row's tolerance, then beat its time on 1920x1080 on this machine. This note is the whole hand-off from
solidcv (solidsf-inc, Rust, zip of 2026-10-21, where we saw the scope list). Nobody on this lane reads its
source. Its speed claims came from Apple M-series and NVIDIA GB-series hardware and are not targets.

## Ideas worth keeping, in our words
- Bit-plane slicing: an 8-bit image as 8 binary planes makes threshold + count-nonzero and frame diffs into
  XOR/AND/popcount. We already have frame block diffs in `~/iTools/media/blockdiff`.
- ORB: FAST corners, orientation by intensity centroid, 256-bit rotated BRIEF, matched by Hamming distance
  (popcount of XOR), 2-NN ratio test.
- PnP: minimal 4-point EPnP hypotheses inside RANSAC with adaptive stopping
  k = ceil(ln(1-p) / ln(1-w^4)), then Gauss-Newton on SO(3) x R^3 (Lie algebra updates) over the inliers.
- Stereo by semi-global matching.

## Primary sources (read these, not solidcv)
- OpenCV 5.0 documentation; its function pages define the semantics we match.
- Rublee et al. 2011 (ORB); Rosten & Drummond 2006 (FAST); Calonder et al. 2010 (BRIEF).
- Lucas & Kanade 1981; Bouguet 2000 (pyramidal LK); Farneback 2003.
- Canny 1986; Suzuki & Abe 1985 (contours); Shi & Tomasi 1994; Rother et al. 2004 (GrabCut);
  Dalal & Triggs 2005 (HOG).
- Lepetit, Moreno-Noguer, Fua 2009 (EPnP); Fischler & Bolles 1981 (RANSAC); Hartley & Zisserman, Multiple
  View Geometry; Nister 2004 (5-point); Hirschmuller 2008 (SGM); Zhang 2000 (calibration);
  Garrido-Jurado et al. 2014 (ArUco).

## Target, frozen before building
Per op, 1920x1080 input: output within tolerance of cv2 5.0 and wall time <= cv2's (best of 5, same thread
count), on CPU or GPU, whichever measures faster. An op that misses the time goes into `docs/bench.md` with
both numbers.
- Stewenius, Engels & Nister 2006, *Recent developments on direct relative orientation*, ISPRS J. 60(4):284-294,
  [doi:10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2006.03.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2006.03.005): the action-matrix variant
  of Nister 2004 used for F3. Same essential cubic constraints; real roots extracted from a 10x10 action matrix.
