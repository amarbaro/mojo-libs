# Provenance: hashed octrees make scene diffs cost the change, not the scene

A 64^3 voxel scene with one changed cell must be diffed by visiting under 1% of the nodes a dense scan visits.
That is the frozen speed bar for this lane. Everything else here is checked for correctness against a
brute-force Mojo oracle or a closed form. This note is the whole hand-off from solidcv (solidsf-inc, Rust, zip
of 2026-10-21, where we saw these ideas). Part of solidcv ships encrypted. Nobody on this lane reads any of it.

## Ideas, in our words
- Voxelize a point cloud to integer cells, and back, keeping each point's residual inside its cell.
- Zobrist hashing: a random 64-bit key per coordinate value, XOR-combined per occupied cell. An octree node's
  hash is the XOR of its children, so two scenes differ only under nodes whose hashes differ.
- Static vs moving: across a short window of frames, cells whose hash stays put are static scene.
- Similarity alignment of two point sets (rotation, translation, scale) in closed form.
- 3D Haar wavelet compression of a static voxel grid: keep coefficients above a threshold.
- Binary 3D point descriptors: signs of local geometric tests packed into bits, matched by popcount.
- TAPVid-3D tracking metrics.
- Phase correlation for frame-to-frame translation; burst super-resolution by registering frames with it,
  splatting onto a finer grid and normalizing.
- CAD: least-squares B-spline / NURBS surface fit on clamped uniform knots, point and normal evaluation; voxel
  boundary faces to a watertight triangle mesh (welded vertices, every edge on exactly two faces, Euler
  characteristic checked); GLB (glTF 2.0 binary) and STEP AP242 (ISO 10303-21) writers.
- CAE: from a 3x3 stress tensor, hydrostatic part, von Mises stress, principal values, value-to-colour map.

## Primary sources (read these, not solidcv)
- Zobrist 1970; Meagher 1982 (octrees).
- Umeyama 1991 (least-squares similarity between point patterns).
- Mallat, A Wavelet Tour of Signal Processing; Kuglin & Hines 1975 (phase correlation).
- Koppula et al. 2024 (TAPVid-3D); Wronski et al. 2019 (handheld burst super-resolution).
- Piegl & Tiller, The NURBS Book; Khronos glTF 2.0 specification; ISO 10303-21 and the AP242 schema.
- Any continuum mechanics text for von Mises and principal stresses.

## Checks, frozen before building
Every module: brute-force or closed-form oracle passes. GLB: read back by our reader, Khronos validator if
installed. STEP: read back by our parser for the entities we write. Octree diff node count printed against the
dense count.
